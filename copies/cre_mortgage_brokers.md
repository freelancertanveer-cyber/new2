# CRE Mortgage Brokers — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `cre_mortgage_brokers_merged`

**Why this one first (over the other 6):**

- Every commercial mortgage on the planet has a fixed maturity date printed on the note. That means a *debt-maturity signal* is not a "nice angle" for CRE brokers — it is literally their entire sales pipeline. 1:1 correlation between signal and buying intent.
- TAM is massive and fragmented: thousands of independent CRE brokerages / mortgage shops per state, plus loan officers running their own book. Every metro has 50–500 of them on Google Maps.
- Deal size is huge (a single refi commission is $10k–$150k+), so they can afford $2k–$5k/month for leads without blinking.
- Their competitors are all buying the same LinkedIn/Apollo-scraped list of "CRE professionals." Google Maps gives us the small independent shops and loan officer LLCs that are **not** in those DBs.
- The niche is saturated on the *broker's* side (every broker is chasing the same borrower), which is exactly why our "volume + fresh data beats precision" thesis lands — they already know precision alone is not saving them.
- The 10-signal "maturing debt / bridge-loan / balloon coming due" data play is a story most of them have never been pitched cleanly. Zero pattern-fatigue.

## The offer stack we are pitching (in order)

1. **Front-end (easy yes):** 25–50 sample borrower-profile records with debt-maturity signal — free, no call, no deposit.
2. **Pilot:** 100–300 verified companies with upcoming debt maturity + finance decision-maker contact — flat fee.
3. **Full campaign (unlocked after trust):** Google-Maps-sourced borrower data + fully-managed cold email infra doing ~500k sends/month from pre-warmed inboxes, week-1 live, priced at low infra cost.

The first two emails only sell #1. #3 shows up in email 3 and in reply threads.

---

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer/vendor/client, sparks curiosity, does not telegraph "cold email," relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Specific pain → unique mechanism → tiny proof.
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / send it / sure." No calendar. No 30-min call ask.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in."
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers.
- No CTA that requires them to think, schedule, or write more than one word.
- Front-end offer carries the unique mechanism (Google Maps + debt-maturity signal) — not the back-end service.
- Psyche match: CRE broker language ("your borrower pipeline," "refi book," "balloon coming due," "bridge take-out").
