# Private Credit Brokers — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `private_credit_brokers_growth_stage`

**Why this one next (over the remaining 3):**

- Private debt maturity and the need for restructuring or capital injection is the **single biggest trigger for "let's explore alternative capital options"** — a company with $5M–$50M in private debt or structured credit coming due in 18–36 months is already running scenario planning. Private credit brokers own the complex placement, and being top-of-mind 12 months early is everything.
- TAM is solid and fragmented: hundreds of independent private credit shops, alternative finance brokers, structured debt specialists, and family office advisors across the US. Every metro has 20–100 actively hunting deal flow.
- Deal size and commissions are enormous ($50k–$500k+ per placement), so they can absolutely afford $2k–$5k/month for high-signal proprietary deal pipeline.
- Mid-market founders and CFOs holding complex private debt are *underrepresented* in traditional broker lists vs. venture-backed companies — but they are visible in business databases, SBA/ESOP records, and corporate filings. Fresh data on this cohort = sourcing advantage.
- The broker side is starved for *proprietary* deal flow at the upper end. Most private credit sourcing runs through existing relationships, bankers, and venture networks — we hand them companies planning capital moves that haven't engaged advisors yet. Zero competition, zero pattern fatigue.
- The debt-maturity signal works *better* here than in most niches because the decision isn't "should we explore this lender" — it's "we have to solve a capital structure problem in 18 months." That is a strategic conversation that only happens once every 5 years per company.

## The offer stack we are pitching (in order)

1. **Front-end (easy yes):** 15–25 founder/CFO profiles with upcoming private debt maturity + cap table snapshot and estimated revenue — free, no call, no deposit.
2. **Pilot:** 50–150 verified mid-market companies with significant debt due in 18–36 months, complete founder/CFO contact data, board composition, recent funding history — flat fee.
3. **Full campaign (unlocked after trust):** Database + industry-research-sourced mid-market company profiles + highly-targeted outbound from founder-voice emails, 50–100 outreach per week (not 400k blasts), 1–2 qualified capital-move convos per week expected, priced at advisory retainer.

The first two emails only sell #1. #3 shows up in email 3 and in reply threads.

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video, adapted for advisory)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap with advisory tone**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer/industry advisor/fellow traveler (not a "growth hacker"), sparks curiosity, does not telegraph cold email, relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Specific capital challenge → strategic mechanism → tiny proof from similar situation.
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / interested / let's talk." No hard calendar push on first touch. "Calendar" is a later-stage ask.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in."
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers. No growth-hacker language.
- No CTA that requires them to think, schedule, or write more than one word (except "interested" on warmer touches).
- Front-end offer carries the unique mechanism (rare capital-structure data + debt-maturity signal) — not the back-end advisory.
- Psyche match: Private credit language ("capital structure," "restructuring," "debt maturity," "growth capital," "founder," "board," "strategic alternatives," "exit readiness").
- Tone: peer-to-peer, advisory mindset, not transactional. No "leads."

## Implementation notes

- **Capital-structure data sources:** SEC Edgar filings, cap-table databases, commercial credit bureaus, SBA loan registries, venture databases (Crunchbase, PitchBook — for founder tracking), state business filings, regulatory records.
- **Overlap with existing campaigns:** If running this alongside SBA or equipment finance, segment by company size (private credit targets $5M+ debt, not $500k loans). No overlap if you segment by deal sophistication.
- **Quality bar:** Only include companies with verifiable debt maturity dates within 18–36 months. Must have visible cap-table data (founders, board, recent funding). Prioritize founder-led over PE-backed (PE already has relationships). No "estimated" maturity guesses.
- **Contact validation:** Always pull founder/CFO direct email from LinkedIn, corporate website, or SEC filings. For board members, validate contact via official records. Skip generic "info@" addresses — this is advisory outreach, not volume blasting.
- **Tone in outreach:** Founder-to-founder or advisor-to-advisor voice. Never "growth hacker" language. Focus on strategic timing and capital structure, not "leads" or "deal volume."
