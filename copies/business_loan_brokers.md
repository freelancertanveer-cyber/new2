# Business Loan Brokers — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `business_loan_brokers_sme`

**Why this one next (over the remaining 5):**

- Loan maturity is the **single biggest trigger for "let's explore refinancing options"** — a $500k–$5M small business loan with 24–36 months left is actively on a business owner's radar. Business loan brokers own the origination, and timing is everything.
- TAM is massive and fragmented: thousands of independent business loan shops, SBA lenders, alternative finance brokers, and loan originators across the US. Every metro has 100+ on Google Maps + LinkedIn that are actively hunting deal flow.
- Origination fees are substantial ($10k–$75k per placement), so they can absolutely afford $1.5k–$4k/month for high-signal borrower pipeline.
- SME owners holding $500k–$5M in business debt are *underrepresented* in traditional Apollo/ZoomInfo datasets vs. corporate buyers — but they are visible on Google Maps, business registries, SBA loan records, and state filings. Fresh data = origination advantage.
- The broker side is starved for *proprietary* deal flow. Lenders and brokers all fight over the same LinkedIn borrower profiles — we hand them companies that haven't listed their refinancing intent anywhere yet. Zero pattern fatigue.
- The debt-maturity signal works *better* here than in many niches because the decision isn't "should we explore this lender" — it's "we *have* to refinance something in 18 months." That is a deal-ready conversation.

## The offer stack we are pitching (in order)

1. **Front-end (easy yes):** 25–50 borrower-profile records with upcoming loan maturity + estimated revenue and borrowing history — free, no call, no deposit.
2. **Pilot:** 100–300 verified SME companies with debt maturing in 12–36 months, full business + owner contact data, finance/operations contact info — flat fee.
3. **Full campaign (unlocked after trust):** Google-Maps + business-registry-sourced SME borrower profiles + fully-managed outbound deal-flow pipeline doing ~450k sends/month from pre-warmed inboxes, 2–3 qualified borrower convos per week expected, priced at low infra cost.

The first two emails only sell #1. #3 shows up in email 3 and in reply threads.

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer/vendor/fellow deal-maker, sparks curiosity, does not telegraph "cold email," relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Specific pain → unique mechanism → tiny proof.
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / send it / sure." No calendar. No 30-min call ask.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in."
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers.
- No CTA that requires them to think, schedule, or write more than one word.
- Front-end offer carries the unique mechanism (SME borrower profiles + debt-maturity trigger) — not the back-end service.
- Psyche match: Business loan broker language ("your borrower pipeline," "origination book," "refi ready," "loan maturity," "deal flow," "business owners").

## Implementation notes

- **Loan-maturity data sources:** Public SBA loan registers, state UCC filings, business credit bureau records, commercial loan marketplaces, state business filings.
- **Overlap with existing campaigns:** If running this alongside CRE or M&A advisory, segment by geography and business type first — many owners operate in multiple niches, which is fine if timing is staggered >2 weeks.
- **Quality bar:** Only include businesses with verifiable debt maturity dates within 12–36 months. No "estimated" maturity guesses. Prioritize SBA loans and documented commercial lines of credit.
- **Contact validation:** Always validate owner/CFO/finance email via company website + LinkedIn + state filings before sending. Skip profiles where you can only find a generic "info@" or "hello@" address.
