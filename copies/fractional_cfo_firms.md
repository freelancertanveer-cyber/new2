# Fractional CFO Firms — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `fractional_cfo_growth_stage`

**Why this one next (over the remaining 1):**

- Debt maturity is the **biggest catalyst for "we need deeper CFO support"** — a founder with $500k–$5M in debt coming due in 12–24 months needs financial scenario planning, which is exactly what fractional CFOs do. This trigger expands the TAM and unlocks upsell opportunities for existing clients.
- TAM is massive and fragmented: thousands of fractional CFO practices, virtual CFO shops, accounting-plus-CFO firms, and one-person CFO consultants across the US. Every metro has 50–300 actively managing SME finances.
- Monthly retainers are $1.5k–$10k per client, so they can afford $800–$2.5k/month for high-signal client sourcing (or upsell intelligence for existing clients).
- Founders actively planning debt maturity scenarios are *underrepresented* in typical growth-stage leads but *overrepresented* in business registries, SBA loan records, and public filings. Fresh data on this cohort = client acquisition advantage.
- The fractional CFO space is starved for *strategic* sourcing. Most acquisition runs through inbound referrals or LinkedIn — we hand them founders who are actively modeling "what if we refinance or restructure." Zero pattern fatigue.
- The debt-maturity signal works *better* here than in many niches because fractional CFOs are already the natural advisor for that conversation. It's not a "pitch cold lender" play — it's "help your clients prepare for debt maturity" which is exactly their job.

1. **Front-end (easy yes):** 15–25 founder profiles with upcoming debt maturity + current cash flow and estimated cash-position stress — free, no call, no deposit. Angle: "Help your clients plan for this."
2. **Pilot:** 50–100 verified founder-led companies with debt due in 12–24 months, complete financial + founder contact data, cash flow health assessment — flat fee.
3. **Full campaign (unlocked after trust):** List-building + founder-targeted outreach + lead delivery, 40–75 new prospects per month with financial health profiles, monthly retainer (client sourcing service) or revenue share on converted retainers.

The first two emails only sell #1. #3 shows up in email 3 and in reply threads.

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video, advisor adaptation)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap with practitioner mindset**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer/fellow CFO advisor, sparks professional relevance, does not telegraph cold email, relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Client challenge (debt maturity + cash flow stress) → CFO mechanism (scenario planning) → proof from similar client situation.
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / interested / send it." No hard CTA push; practitioners respect your time.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in."
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers. Only practitioner-to-practitioner language.
- No CTA that requires them to think hard. Fractional CFOs are busy running client books.
- Front-end offer carries the unique mechanism (debt-maturity founder profiles + cash-flow analysis) — not the back-end service.
- Psyche match: Fractional CFO language ("client growth," "cash flow planning," "debt maturity," "refinancing scenario," "founder financial planning," "capital structure," "retainer client").
- Tone: peer advisor, problem-solver, respectful of their client relationships and time. No "leads," only "prospects" or "high-signal opportunities."

## Implementation notes

- **Founder + debt data sources:** Business registries, SBA loan databases, public credit records, industry financial benchmarks (if available), LinkedIn research (for founder validation), company website financial data, news/press releases.
- **Overlap with existing campaigns:** If running this alongside business loan brokers or equipment finance, segment by founder intention. Business loan brokers target borrowers actively refinancing; CFOs target founders planning cash-flow scenarios. Different messaging, same data layer — can run parallel if messaging is segmented.
- **Quality bar:** Only include founders with verifiable debt maturity within 12–24 months AND visible cash-flow information (revenue trend, growth rate, debt service coverage). Prioritize founders showing financial stress or mature-stage cash flow (past hockey-stick growth). No "estimated" data.
- **Contact validation:** Always pull founder direct email from LinkedIn, company website, or business registries. Skip generic "contact us" forms or main office numbers — you need founder/CFO level contact. For advisory relationships, validate through website or LinkedIn profile.
- **Tone in outreach:** CFO-to-CFO voice. Focus on client benefit (debt maturity planning, cash-flow scenario work) not lead volume. Respect their client relationships and their time. Position as sourcing partner, not vendor.
