# M&A Advisory Firms — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `m_and_a_advisory_middle_market`

**Why this one next (over the other verticals):**

- Debt maturity is the **single biggest trigger for "strategic alternatives"** — a company with $5M–$50M in debt maturing in 12–24 months has three paths: refinance, sell, or restructure. M&A advisors own the middle one, and timing is everything.
- TAM is massive and fragmented: thousands of independent M&A shops, boutique investment banks, and partnership-model advisory firms across the US. Every mid-market metro has dozens on Google Maps + LinkedIn that are hungry for deal flow.
- Deal commissions are enormous ($250k–$2M+ per transaction), so they can absolutely afford $3k–$7k/month for high-signal deal pipeline.
- Owner-operators holding $5M–$100M in commercial debt are *underrepresented* on LinkedIn/Apollo vs. CRE borrowers — but they are visible on Google Maps, corporate filings, and SBA loan registries. Fresh data = deal-flow advantage.
- The advisory side is starved for *proprietary* deal flow. Brokers and financial advisors all fight over the same 500 LinkedIn "looking to sell" profiles — we hand them companies that haven't listed their intent anywhere yet. Zero pattern fatigue.
- The debt-maturity signal works *better* here than in CRE because the decision isn't "who do we refinance with" — it's "do we actually want to own this business in 3 years?" That is an M&A conversation.

---

## The offer stack we are pitching (in order)

1. **Front-end (easy yes):** 25–50 owner-operated company profiles with upcoming debt maturity + estimated EBITDA and ownership structure — free, no call, no deposit.
2. **Pilot:** 100–300 verified SME companies with debt maturing in 12–24 months, full ownership + contact data, finance/founder contact info — flat fee.
3. **Full campaign (unlocked after trust):** Google-Maps + corporate-records sourced SME profiles + fully-managed outbound deal-flow pipeline doing ~400k sends/month from pre-warmed inboxes, 2–3 qualified seller convos per week expected, priced at low infra cost.

The first two emails only sell #1. #3 shows up in email 3 and in reply threads.

---

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer/advisor/fellow deal-doer, sparks curiosity, does not telegraph "cold email," relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Specific pain → unique mechanism → tiny proof.
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / send it / sure." No calendar. No 30-min call ask.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in."
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers.
- No CTA that requires them to think, schedule, or write more than one word.
- Front-end offer carries the unique mechanism (SME owner profiles + debt-maturity trigger) — not the back-end service.
- Psyche match: M&A advisor language ("deal flow," "exit-ready companies," "strategic alternatives," "refinance vs. sale," "SME pipeline," "owner-operator sellers").

## Implementation notes

- **Debt-maturity data sources:** Public SBA loan registers, state UCC filings, SEC Edgar filings (for companies with public debt), commercial mortgage registries.
- **Overlap with existing campaigns:** If running this alongside a different advisory vertical (e.g., debt restructuring firms), segment by geography first, then by industry — the same owner may get two different CTAs, which is fine if timing is staggered >2 weeks.
- **Quality bar:** Only include companies with verifiable debt maturity dates within 12–24 months. No "estimated" maturity guesses.
- **Contact validation:** Always validate founder/owner email via company website + LinkedIn + state filings before sending. Skip profiles where you can only find a generic "info@" address.
