# CPA Accounting Firms — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `cpa_accounting_firms_mid_market`

**Why this one last (it's the weakest fit, but still viable):**

- Debt maturity is a **client advisory opportunity for CPAs** — business owners often turn to their CPA when modeling financial scenarios, and "what happens when our debt comes due" is a natural conversation. CPAs can add advisory value + referral relationships.
- TAM is massive (hundreds of thousands of CPAs in the US), but the buying intent is indirect. CPAs aren't the primary deal-makers for restructuring/refinancing — they're gatekeepers and advisors who influence those conversations.
- Revenue per CPA engagement is lower ($500–$2k/month for referral partnerships) than other niches, but TAM scale and existing client relationships make it viable as a referral + upsell play.
- Business owners with upcoming debt maturity are *clearly visible* in CPA client portfolios (through tax returns, balance sheets, cash-flow analysis). CPAs have early-signal data but no systematized way to act on it.
- The CPA side is starved for *credible referral partners* in restructuring, private credit, and M&A advisory. Most referrals come through networks or bank relationships — we hand them vetted advisors they can confidently refer to.
- The debt-maturity signal works *differently* here than in other niches: CPAs don't close deals themselves, but they unlock deal sourcing for other advisors (restructuring, private credit, M&A) through referral relationships. It's a B2B2B play.

## The offer stack we are pitching (in order)

1. **Front-end (easy yes):** 10–15 client-profile templates + guidance docs on "how to have the debt-maturity conversation with your clients" — free, no call, positions you as advisory resource.
2. **Pilot:** Proprietary list of 50–100 verified companies with debt maturity + verified contact data, sent to your referral partners, shared revenue on referrals — flat fee or revenue share trial.
3. **Full campaign (unlocked after trust):** Ongoing proprietary-deal referral network (CPAs as sourcing layer for restructuring/private credit/M&A advisors), revenue share on referrals + closed deals, or retainer for premium referral placement.

The first two emails only sell #1. #3 shows up in email 3 and in reply threads.

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video, referral partner adaptation)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap with advisor-network mindset**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer advisor/fellow business professional, positions you as resource (not vendor), does not telegraph cold email, relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Client challenge (debt maturity + advisory gap) → advisory mechanism (CPA-led conversation) → proof (like-minded CPAs using this).
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / interested / send it." Low-pressure; referral partnerships work on trust, not hard sells.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in."
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers. Only peer-to-peer professional language.
- No CTA that requires them to change their business model. CPAs are busy; make this easy.
- Front-end offer carries the unique mechanism (client-conversation guidance + proprietary deal data) — not the back-end service.
- Psyche match: CPA language ("client advisory," "financial planning," "debt maturity," "scenario modeling," "referral partner," "professional network").
- Tone: peer advisor, problem-solver, respectful of CPA client relationships, positioned as resource + referral partner, not vendor.

## Implementation notes

- **Client debt-maturity identification:** Pull from CPA's own client financial statements (balance sheets, cash flow, debt schedules), cross-reference with public records (SBA loans, UCC filings), or use disclosed information from clients.
- **Referral partner sourcing:** We manage the referral partner network on behalf of CPAs. Ensure all partners are vetted (restructuring shops, private credit brokers, M&A advisors) and have clear referral agreements (10–15% of first-year fees is standard).
- **Overlap with existing campaigns:** If running this alongside other niches, position CPAs as a sourcing layer (B2B2B) for restructuring/private credit/M&A advisors. CPAs don't close deals; they source + refer.
- **Quality bar:** Only connect CPAs with referral partners who have (a) clear referral fee structure, (b) track record of closing deals, (c) professionalism + client-relationship respect. No low-quality lenders or high-pressure firms.
- **Tone in outreach:** CPA-to-CPA voice. Position as peer advisor + resource, not vendor. Respect their client relationships above all. Referral partnerships work on trust; build slowly.
- **Key difference:** This niche is about building referral networks + advisory advisory, not direct lead sales. CPAs are intermediaries, not buyers.
