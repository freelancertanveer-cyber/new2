# Debt Advisory & Restructuring — Cold Email Copy Pack

**Industry pick:** `debt_advisory_restructuring_distressed`

**Why this one next (over the remaining 2):**

- Debt maturity in distressed or over-leveraged situations is the **single most time-sensitive trigger for "we need professional help right now"** — a company with $10M–$100M+ in debt due in 12–18 months with refinancing challenges has already burned capital on internal solutions and is actively hunting restructuring advisors. These advisors own the problem-solve, and being top-of-mind at month 6 before due date is everything.
- TAM is solid but focused: hundreds of independent restructuring shops, debt advisory boutiques, turnaround specialists, and distressed-finance advisors across major metros. Each has a 20–50 person team actively building a distressed deal pipeline.
- Engagement fees are enormous ($100k–$1M+ per restructuring), so they can absolutely afford $2k–$6k/month for high-signal distressed company pipeline.
- Over-leveraged and distressed CFOs are *highly visible* in public filings (debt covenants, credit agreements, 8-K filings, industry news) but *underrepresented* in typical broker/advisor networks until the situation is critical. Early signal = engagement advantage.
- The advisor side is starved for *early warning* deal flow. Most distressed situations come through bank referrals or legal firm introductions — we hand them companies with brewing capital structure problems that haven't yet engaged advisors. Zero competition on discovery.
- The debt-maturity + covenant-stress signal works *better* here than in any other niche because the decision isn't "should we explore options" — it's "we *have* to solve this or we're in default." That is a crisis conversation that compresses a 12-month sales cycle into 3 months.

## Framework applied (from the Lead Gen Jay video, crisis adaptation)

Every sequence below is engineered against the **Triple-Tap with crisis mindset**:

1. **Open** — Preview text (subject + first line) sounds like a peer advisor/fellow restructuring professional, sparks immediate relevance (not curiosity, *relevance*), does not telegraph cold email, relates to the body.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max. Specific debt challenge → restructuring mechanism → proof from similar distressed situation.
3. **Reply** — One-handed "yes / let's talk / send details." Calendar comes naturally for crisis situations; no push needed.

Rules I held to:
- No "quick question," no "hope you're well," no "just checking in" — these are serious situations.
- No generic "I help X do Y" openers. No growth-hacker language. Only restructuring professional voice.
- No CTA that requires them to think. Crisis-stage buyers move fast — simplify everything.
- Front-end offer carries the unique signal (covenant-stress data + debt-maturity combined) — not the back-end advisory.
- Psyche match: Restructuring language ("debt maturity," "covenant breach risk," "refinancing challenge," "leverage ratio," "default risk," "capital structure," "distressed," "turnaround").
- Tone: peer-to-peer, crisis-aware, problem-solver mentality. No "leads," only "situations."

## Implementation notes

- **Debt-maturity + covenant data sources:** SEC Edgar filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K), credit agreement public records, bond prospectus data, bank loan covenant disclosures, Bloomberg terminals (if available), state corporate filings, industry credit-watch services.
- **Overlap with existing campaigns:** If running this alongside private credit or CRE, segment by distress level and company size. Private credit targets growth capital structures; restructuring targets over-leveraged + covenant stress. No overlap if done right.
- **Quality bar:** Only include companies with verifiable debt maturity within 12–24 months AND visible covenant stress signals (leverage ratios >3.5x, interest coverage <2x, recent credit events, declining cash flow). No speculative guesses. Prioritize companies already showing stress, not "might be stressed."
- **Contact validation:** Always pull CFO/controller direct email from SEC filings (proxy statements, executive bios), company website, or LinkedIn. Skip generic "ir@" or "finance@" addresses — this is advisory-level outreach. For advisory firms, validate managing partner/principal contact via law firm or company site.
- **Tone in outreach:** Advisor-to-advisor voice. Focus on signal quality and early-warning advantage, not volume or "leads." Acknowledge that restructuring is serious — match that tone in every email.
