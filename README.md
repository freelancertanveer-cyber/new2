## Framework

Every sequence is built against the **Triple-Tap** (Lead Gen Jay):

1. **Open** — subject + first line does not telegraph cold email, sounds like a peer/vendor/client, sparks curiosity.
2. **Read** — 2–4 sentences max: specific pain → unique mechanism → tiny proof.
3. **Reply** — one-handed "yes / send it." No calendar link on first touch, no 30-min call ask.

Every front-end offer is a *free 25-record sample* keyed to the prospect's own filters. The full managed-campaign offer only shows up in email 3+ and in reply threads.

## Why CRE mortgage brokers first

Full rationale in `copies/cre_mortgage_brokers.md`. Short version:

- Every commercial mortgage has a maturity date on the note → 1:1 fit with our signal.
- TAM is huge and fragmented (thousands of local shops per state on Google Maps).
- Deal commissions are big → they can afford $2k–$5k/month for leads.
- Owner-operator borrowers are underrepresented in LinkedIn/Apollo/CoStar → Google Maps is the actual unlock.
- Broker side is saturated → "volume + fresh data beats precision" thesis lands cleanly.
